LASER DROP

Hide. Aim. Fire. Last one standing wins.

How to play
  1. Roam the arena with everyone until the host hits Start.
  2. Everyone turns invisible. You get 3 seconds to move and aim.
  3. All positions are revealed and every laser fires at once.
  4. Hit = out. The floor shrinks. Repeat until one is left.

Move: WASD / arrows · Aim: mouse

Move: left stick · Aim: right stick
